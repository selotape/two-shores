# Israeli quest catalogue (version 1)

Each entry is a starting point, not a rule. Before relying on any figure or deadline, open the source named
and cite the current page with the date you read it. Sources: Kol Zchut (כל זכות, a well-kept rights guide run by a non-profit, which links to the official rules), the Israel Tax Authority
(רשות המסים), the National Insurance Institute (ביטוח לאומי), the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings
Authority (רשות שוק ההון) and its Har HaKesef search (הר הכסף), and the pension clearing house (המסלקה
הפנסיונית). Written 2026-11-23; every entry is for tax year 2026 unless the source says otherwise.

| # | Quest | Trigger | What a professional would cover | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find every forgotten fund | Always | Search Har HaKesef for dormant pension, provident, hishtalmut and insurance money in each adult's name; before merging or moving any old fund, have a pension advisor check it: old policies (for example pre-2013 executive insurance) can carry guaranteed annuity terms or cover that a merge loses | Capital Market Authority (Har HaKesef) |
| 2 | Map every pension in one report | Any salaried or self-employed adult | Pull the clearing house report; for each fund: balance, track, management fees on deposits and on balance, insurance cover inside it, beneficiaries | Pension clearing house; Kol Zchut |
| 3 | Check pension fees and track | Any pension fund | Compare fees with what the fund offers on request and with default funds; check the investment track fits age; never switch an old policy before an advisor checks what it guarantees | Capital Market Authority |
| 4 | Keren hishtalmut: fill it and know when it frees up | Any employee or self-employed adult | For employees it depends on the employer offering it; deposit up to the tax-exempt ceiling; note the date it becomes withdrawable without tax; don't break it early | Israel Tax Authority; Kol Zchut |
| 5 | Annual tax report: required, or worth filing for a refund | Always (check each year) | Whether a report is required (for example capital gains, foreign income, rent above the exempt level, or income the employer could not withhold for). Two employers at once need tax coordination (תיאום מס) during the year, which is not the same as a report; a refund claim can go back up to 6 years | Israel Tax Authority; Kol Zchut |
| 6 | Tax credit points are all claimed | Any salaried adult | Form 101 with each employer: children, academic degree, single parent, new immigrant and other credit points | Israel Tax Authority; Kol Zchut |
| 7 | Children's savings plan (חיסכון לכל ילד) | Any child under 18 | Choose the fund and track; whether to add the parental top-up from the child allowance | National Insurance Institute |
| 8 | National Insurance status is in order | Self-employed, not working, or abroad for long | Contributions current; rights to benefits not lost | National Insurance Institute |
| 9 | Life cover sized to the gap | Children, a mortgage, or one main earner | Need = debts + years of support − savings − cover already inside the pension; mortgage life insurance priced against the market | Capital Market Authority; Kol Zchut |
| 10 | Disability cover (אובדן כושר עבודה) | Any earner | What the pension fund already covers, whether a gap remains, whether the definition is own-occupation | Capital Market Authority |
| 11 | Health insurance not paid twice | Any private health policy | Overlap between the HMO supplemental plan (שב"ן) and private policies; keep only what closes a real gap | Capital Market Authority; Kol Zchut |
| 12 | Mortgage: track mix and refinance check | A mortgage | Tracks, rates, prepayment fees; whether refinancing (מחזור) pays after fees | Bank of Israel; Kol Zchut |
| 13 | Will, guardians and lasting power of attorney | Children or shared assets | A will each; a written wish for guardians (the court decides, but gives it weight); a lasting power of attorney (ייפוי כוח מתמשך), deposited with the Administrator General | Kol Zchut; the Administrator General (האפוטרופוס הכללי) |
| 14 | Emergency cash and bank fees | Always | Months of spending held in cash; fee track at the bank; cash beyond that put to work | Bank of Israel; Kol Zchut |
| 15 | Investment account reported right | A brokerage account | Capital gains withheld or reported; foreign brokers mean an annual report; losses offset | Israel Tax Authority |

Triggers are checked strictly against the register and interview. If a trigger can't be checked yet, the quest
opens in fog with "find out" as its next step.
